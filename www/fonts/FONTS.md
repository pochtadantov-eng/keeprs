# Fonts

- `TT-Firs-Neue-Trial-Var-Roman.ttf` — TT Firs Neue **Trial** (TypeType). The trial licence covers testing and
  evaluation only; before publishing or distributing the app, replace it with licensed files
  (https://typetype.org/fonts/tt-firs-neue/) or drop it and fall back to Onest.
- `onest-*.woff2` — Onest, SIL OFL 1.1 (`OFL-Onest.txt`), used as the fallback.
